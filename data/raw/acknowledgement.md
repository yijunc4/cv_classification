# Data acknowledgements

The alert catalogue, recorded class labels, and G-band light curves used in this
project come from [Gaia Photometric Science Alerts](https://gsaweb.ast.cam.ac.uk/alerts/).
We acknowledge ESA Gaia, DPAC and the Photometric Science Alerts Team.

The additional astrometric and photometric metadata come from
[Gaia Data Release 3](https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia/data-release-3), accessed
through the [Gaia Archive](https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/).
We thank the ESA Gaia mission, the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium
(DPAC), and the national institutions supporting the Gaia Multilateral Agreement.

The feature tables and cleaned light curves distributed here are derived from
these data. Source documentation and acknowledgement guidance are available from
[Gaia Alerts](https://gsaweb.ast.cam.ac.uk/alerts/docs/) and
[Gaia DR3](https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/documentation/GDR3/Miscellaneous/sec_acknowl/).
